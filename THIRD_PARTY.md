# Dependencies and source attribution

The repository retains the course's original programs and small generated assets. The upstream file manifest is `docs/UPSTREAM_FILES.json`.

The following projects are runtime dependencies, not bundled source libraries:

- Python: https://www.python.org/
- NumPy: https://numpy.org/
- SciPy: https://scipy.org/
- Matplotlib: https://matplotlib.org/
- PyTorch: https://pytorch.org/

Their own distributions provide their license notices. TeX Live, latexmk and Pandoc are only needed for the separately distributed editable course documents.

Research attribution remains in the lecture notes' chapter source notes and references, and in existing code comments. This packaging does not claim the cited research methods as new contributions and does not assign new rights to them. The supplied tasks, arrays and small model weights are the course's saved synthetic experiment artifacts. No external research-paper PDFs are bundled.
